## Answers to the review questions

**Q1 (highest weight) — is C1(c) a self-referential loop, and how do we book it?**

Yes, strictly read, C1(c) is self-referential: "independent recheck required" is false
*before* any recheck happens, and the recheck's conclusion is "is C1 met?" The loop is
only a genuine failure mode when the **author** is the one closing it — that is exactly
the self-sign `XJ-20261003-001` committed. Here the closer is an **independent** reviewer
(I), so closing the loop is not laundering; it is the recheck C1 named. **My chosen
accounting: option (i) — this card's `PASS` is the independent recheck that satisfies C1(c),
and its existence declares C1 resolved.** I do **not** prefer option (ii) (re-issue a new
`PASS` on `XJ-20261003-002` pointing at `e175f58` first), because that just *moves* the
closure to a second card without adding evidence, and it runs into the same-id-duplication
problem Q2 asks about. Option (i) is the minimal, honest closure: one independent verdict
stands behind the resolution.

**Q2 — after a `CONDITIONAL` blocker is cleared, what happens to the original verdict?**

The original `CONDITIONAL` verdict on `XJ-20261003-002` (on `b754ae4`) is **retained as a
historical record — do not overwrite or delete it in place** (two same-id verdict files, or a
silent in-place edit, are both worse than the state they replace). The reusable rule I
propose: **a cleared `CONDITIONAL` is retired by a *distinct* closure reference, not by
re-judging the same id.** Concretely, for this family the closure is:
`XJ-20261004-003` (`PASS`, on `e175f58`) is the recheck that clears `XJ-20261003-002`'s C1;
record that link on the `XJ-20261003-002` verdict side as a `superseded_by` /
`closed_by: "XJ-20261004-003"` annotation (a *separate* field on the historical file or a
closure note), **without** minting a second `XJ-20261003-002.verdict.json`.
Note that §6.1 **supersession is a freshness mechanism** (a later *valid, fresh* authority on
the *same file* retires an earlier STALE finding) — it is **not** a mechanism for
"re-judging a cleared CONDITIONAL." Forcing that onto §6.1 would be a category error. The
honest answer: **re-judging a CONDITIONAL is a documented exception that needs its own ADR
(card-schema change: a `closed_by` / `superseded_by` link), exactly the kind of change §6.2
P5 and §8 already flag as "waits for its own ADR."** Until that ADR exists, the minimal
workable practice is the closure note above. This gives at least two cards
(`XJ-20261003-001`, `XJ-20261003-002`) one reusable rule.

**Q3 — does "the author's self-report of an independent recheck" count as evidence?**

**No.** A commit message saying "I independently verified this" is a self-assertion by the
party the rule exists to keep out of the decision. The same self-report shape appears in
this repo in **three** places:
1. **The `independent` field itself** — it is a free boolean anyone can set `true`; nothing
   in the current gate/lint derives it from the relationship between *verifier* and *commit
   author*, so it is only as trustworthy as the person who filled it in.
2. **§6.2 P5** — the protocol explicitly admits *itself* was committed without an independent
   verdict ("It carries the same debt it describes") — a standing, documented instance of
   the same self-attested gap.
3. **`XJ-20261003-001`** — `verifier: jarvis@agent-covenant` with `independent: true`, while
   the §6.2 text that verdict signed was *written by that same party*. That is a self-sign
   (verifier == author of the judged material), the documented anti-pattern.

**Minimal fix (no big refactor, just landable):**
- **Make `independent` a *derived*, not *asserted*, field for self-signs:** add one machine
  check (lint or gate) that, for the reviewed commit, rejects — or at minimum hard-warns on —
  any verdict where `verifier` equals the commit author's identity **and** `independent:
  true`. In plain words: *you cannot self-sign independence.* This single rule kills the
  whole class (items 1 and 3) at the source without touching the shape of the field.
- **Re-flag `XJ-20261003-001`** as `self_signed: true` / `independent: false` so it stops
  being cited as an independent authority (leave the file; just correct the flag the way
  Q2's closure note corrects a cleared CONDITIONAL).
- **P5 stays as written** (it is an honest debt statement); the fix above is what lets P5's
  debt be *closed* rather than perpetually inherited.
