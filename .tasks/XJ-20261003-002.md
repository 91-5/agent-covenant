### 作者已发现的具体疑点（请独立核实，不要采信）

1. **`VALID_VERDICTS` 是死代码。** 函数内定义了 `("PASS","CONDITIONAL","FAIL")`，
   但实际校验走的是 gate 的 `_check_verdict_value(data)`。这个本地常量**从头到尾没被
   比较过**。这比「重复信源」更糟：它看起来像检查点，实际改了不会有任何影响 ——
   下一个维护者极可能去"修"它。
2. **`--no-verdict-schema` 是无声总开关。** 带该 flag，整项 schema 检查消失，
   **输出里不留任何痕迹**。对比同一函数的 import 失败分支 —— 那里老老实实发了
   `VERDICT_SCHEMA_GATE_IMPORT` ERROR。前者防得住，后者留了个静默关掉的门：
   CI 里误加一个 flag，就会**什么都不查还报绿**。
3. **「lint 与 gate 永不漂移」这句承诺没有兑现。** docstring 写
   `so lint and gate never drift`，但 `ts` 只查了**能否解析**，没查**是不是未来时间**；
   而 gate.py 专门有一道未来时间检查（理由：未来时间的 verdict 永不过期）。
   两边已经分叉，恰是它自己承诺不会发生的那件事。
4. **`independent` 硬编码 `true`**，而 gate.py 提供 `--allow-nonindependent`。
   代码注释说明这是**故意的**（lint 无此开关）。方向相反的分叉（lint 更严），
   请评审者判断这个取舍是否成立，而不是默认接受。

## About PROTOCOL.md and the freeze facilities

**The section above must stay a bare path list.** `lint_cards._owned_files()` takes every
non-empty line of the `## Owned files` section **verbatim** as one ownership claim - it does
not parse paths - so any explanatory prose, including a blockquote, becomes "this card owns
this sentence" and produces a false OWNED_FILES_CONFLICT. This trap cost four findings here:
rewording the prose did not help, only moving it out of the section did.

**`PROTOCOL.md` is deliberately absent from the list above.** It is currently held by
`XJ-20261003-001`; two live cards owning one path is the lost-update hazard the linter calls
OWNED_FILES_CONFLICT (PROTOCOL.md R2). The one line this card wants to add there - that a
card's authority is bounded by its own repository - must wait until XJ-20261003-001 has a
verdict and closes, then be handed to its next owner. Do not write it early.

**`.freeze\` and `.freeze-snap\` are protection facilities. No task may modify or delete them.**
