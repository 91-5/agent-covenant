## Response to author's 7 review questions

The author asked seven questions in `.tasks/XJ-20261003-001.md §Review questions`. My answers, in order:

1. **Q1 — is P1 too strict?** Yes, for typo-only docs commits with no `Acceptance` figures, a full verdict gate is over-kill. A scoped exception ("commits that change only prose and have no numeric or normative claim are exempt") would keep §6.2 honest without blocking corrections. **Advisory**, not a blocker — §6.2 can ship and the exception can be a later round, since the existing P5 ("listed in the next review round") already legitimises retrospective fixes as long as they declare the gap. Recommend opening a card.
2. **Q2 — does P2+P5 normalise "merge first, review later"?** Yes — the phrasing "listed in the next review round instead of pretending otherwise" reads as permission, not as debt. Two readers can read it the opposite way. Sharper wording would say "**§6.2 itself may not be used to reject any third party's submission until this card produces a PASS verdict**" — making the temporary non-enforceability explicit instead of implying acceptance. **Advisory**; can be revisited.
3. **Q3 — what is P3 worth if it's not machine-checkable?** The rule itself is correct and valuable as a reasoning anchor; the current text does not state the machine-checkable limit. Suggest adding one sentence: "this rule is enforceable only against the artifact map and the gate; it is not enforceable against arbitrary squashed merges, which the reviewer inspects by reading the chain." **Advisory**.
4. **Q4 — does P4 overreach?** Mostly fine; the wording "未经验证的数字" leaves "unverified" ambiguous (unverified by whom, by what method?). Recommend sharpening to "Any figure in `Acceptance` that is not reproducible from the `evidence` section is a blocker." **Advisory**.
5. **Q5 — should §6.2 live under §6 or under a new §7?** It belongs under §6. §6 is "the gate", §6.1 is the supersession explanation, §6.2 is the boundary condition. Splitting it would orphan the supersession context. **No change needed.**
6. **Q6 — does §6.2 retroactively judge the repo's prior pushes?** Yes — by the same logic P5 applies to §6.2 itself, any earlier push in this repo that was reviewed after the fact (shim hardening, multi-point snapshot, etc.) is equally "rejected" by §6.2. The text does not address this. Recommend either a one-line "this rule does not retroactively invalidate prior releases — see CHANGELOG entries [0.1.x]" or a `postmortems.md` PM-13 noting the limited reach. **Advisory**.
7. **Q7 — is the "linter cannot see origin/main" argument sound?** Sound. The reasoning is correct: a static working-tree reader cannot see a push event, so the check is structurally out of scope for `lint_cards.py`. However the *Not machine-checkable* entry proposes "pre-push hook or CI" as alternatives without committing to either — that is honest hedging, not a gap. **No change needed**, but the alternative decision should be picked up in a follow-up card (pre-push hook is the cheaper one and lives where the reviewer can author-test it locally).

None of Q1–Q7 are blockers. The only blocker is B1 (cross-reference correctness), which is in the cited text rather than in the rules themselves.
