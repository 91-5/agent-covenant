### Two predictions that turned out wrong (kept, because they were wrong usefully)

This card originally predicted that the gate would block the round-1 verdict with
`FUTURE_VERDICT`. It did not, and the reason was more valuable than the
prediction:

1. Ximo's `ts` was in the future *when written*; by the time we ran the check
   the clock had caught up, so the rule correctly stopped firing. Timestamp rules
   are only as stable as their calibration.
2. We had not passed `--artifact-map`, so freshness never ran at all — and the
   gate said `PASS` with no complaint. **That** is the finding worth keeping: a
   verifier that silently drops its strongest check is worse than no verifier.

Both became code: `FUTURE_VERDICT` + `--max-clock-skew` (condition-adjacent fix
from PM-9) and the `NOTICE` path for uncovered ids (PM-10), plus `artifacts.json`
at the repo root so this repository gates itself in the strong mode by default.

The correct gate result for this round is `STALE_VERDICT` — round 1's verdict
cannot sign round 2, because the artifacts moved after it was written. That is
the protocol working, and it is why a fresh verdict is required below.
