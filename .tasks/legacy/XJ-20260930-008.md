# TASK XJ-20260930-008: disclose that the reviewers in verdicts/ are AI, and that independent: true is a claim rather than a verified fact

## Context

The repository goes public. Every verdict in `verdicts/` carries a `verifier` of the
form `ximo@agnes-ai`, and every one of them carries `independent: true`. Read from the
outside — by someone who has just cloned the repo and is deciding whether to trust the
evidence chain — `independent: true` reads as a *property of the evidence*. It is not.

`PROTOCOL.md` §10.3 already admits that the gate cannot verify independence: nothing in
the format distinguishes a reviewer who genuinely did not touch the work from one who
did. That admission lives in the protocol, four sections deep. A reader who lands on the
README, sees seven `independent: true` verdicts and a gate that enforces them, has every
reason to conclude the property was checked. It was not.

This is the defect class this project exists to catch, in the one direction that is
easiest to miss. Round 006 was a false claim that *oversold* the work. This one
*undersells a limitation*, which is the direction people mistake for modesty. The v0.1.4
README correction was instructive for exactly this reason: the previous statement was
false in the direction that undersells the work, and it survived review anyway.

So the claim surface has to say it, and it has to say it in the README, not only in
`PROTOCOL.md` §10.3. A limitation documented only where the author put it is not
disclosed.

## Deliverables

- `README.md` — a short, unmissable statement, in the Status section, that:
  - the reviewer recorded in `verdicts/` is an AI reviewer (`ximo@agnes-ai`), not a human;
  - `independent: true` is a **claim the reviewer makes about itself**, which no tool in
    this repo can verify;
  - `PROTOCOL.md` §10.3 says so at greater length, and this line exists so a reader does
    not have to go looking for it;
  - the gate verifies that the field is present and truthful *as a string*, not that the
    underlying independence holds
- `README.zh-CN.md` — the same statement in Chinese, **byte-aligned in position** with the
  English one so the two READMEs keep teaching the same thing in the same order (PM-11)
- `artifacts.json` — new `XJ-20260930-008` key naming exactly `README.md` and
  `README.zh-CN.md`
